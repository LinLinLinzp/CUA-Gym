# exp3-05 construction preflight

The three current mocks can support a receiving and billing discrepancy task. NetSuite creates a persisted item receipt and vendor bill from the same purchase order, with stable links to individual PO lines. The bill draws from the PO's cumulative unbilled receipts. There is no direct receipt-to-bill foreign key or separate dispute ledger. Use the PO and line links to reconcile the transactions and, if the user asks for a hold record, use the vendor bill memo or a reply in the existing Mattermost thread.

## Proposed case

Use a new PO number and internal ID with the seeded vendor Summit Power Transmission, Portland warehouse and inventory items from PO4517 as references. The live PO is the already approved revision B. Seed one genuine earlier linked receipt for 8 belts and 4 sprockets and one genuine earlier linked bill for 6 belts and 4 sprockets. Seed opening stock inclusive of that earlier receipt. Two of the earlier belts remain unbilled for a separately identified invoice pending AP processing.

| PO line | Approved revision B | Earlier received / billed | Signed current dock receipt | Current vendor invoice |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| BLT-5VX800 at $38.20 | 18 | 8 / 6 | 10 accepted | 14 claimed |
| SPR-40B18 at $27.40 | 12 | 4 / 4 | 0 accepted | 3 claimed |

Yahoo Mail holds the vendor's current invoice SPT-97641 with invoice date, due date, current shipment identifier, and itemized claims. An earlier order acknowledgment references revision A, which allowed 22 belts. Mattermost holds a buyer message confirming approved revision B reduced the belt line to 18, a preliminary dock tally echoing the invoice counts, and a later reply from the warehouse lead signing off on 10 belts and zero accepted sprockets. The rejected sprocket carton never entered available inventory. Give AP a visible instruction authorizing posting the matched portion against the current invoice and tracking the balance as disputed. Tie the earlier two unbilled belts to a separate shipment and AP reference so the vendor invoice cannot absorb them. Put key evidence in the mail and post bodies so the scenario works with ordinary search and requires no upload or external communication.

## Workflow and grading

- On the open PO, choose **Receive**. Both eligible lines default selected and filled to remaining PO quantity. Keep the belt line at 10 and uncheck the sprocket line. Save and retain the new receipt ID.
- From that PO choose **Bill**. Its default is 12 belts because the form includes the two previously received and unbilled. Set the belt quantity to 10, enter SPT-97641 in **Reference No.**, and confirm the invoice date and due date. Changing date or terms recalculates the due date. Rates come from the PO. The correct new open bill is $382.00 with $0 paid and $382.00 remaining. The disputed current invoice balance is $235.00, comprising 4 belts and 3 sprockets.
- A valid alternative is to select the same PO from the standalone Item Receipt or Vendor Bill source picker. Score the saved records irrespective of entry point. A particularly useful negative fixture is a bill saved at the default 12 belts. That bill is valid in NetSuite and totals $458.40, but incorrectly consumes two earlier belts. A further negative fixture is a receipt for 3 sprockets. It is within the open PO balance and can be saved despite lacking warehouse acceptance.
- Compare server snapshots for exactly one new target receipt and one new target bill. Require each child's `createdFromId` to match the PO's string `id`, `createdFromType='purchaseOrder'`, and its sole line's `orderLineId` and `itemId` to match the belt source line. Bill `tranId` is the vendor reference and `transactionNumber` is the generated BILL number. Use the PO and line IDs, as well as vendor reference, when identifying the transactions. Ensure there are no duplicate or compensating inventory, payment or purchasing records for this task.
- Check cumulative quantities received/billed of 18/16 belts and 4/4 sprockets, open sprocket quantity of 8, and PO status `Pending Billing/Partially Received`. Validate that ordered quantities, rates, approval and open line flags match the initial PO, the historical receipt and bill remain intact, and Portland stock rises by exactly 10 belts with zero sprocket increase. In a requested memo or Mattermost reply, grade the held items, amounts and source context. If using a reply, check the precise `channelId`, `rootId` and author.
- Use a fresh sid/context when validating a new injection. Prefer a full seed for baseline comparisons because the startup merge fills unspecified defaults and submits a baseline write. NetSuite caches browser state and posts whole snapshots asynchronously. In QA, verify the receipt in `GET /go?sid=…` before saving the bill and verify the final result in `current_state`. Yahoo Mail read flags and Mattermost viewing cursors can change during research.
