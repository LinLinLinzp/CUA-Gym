## QuickBooks source and vendor counterpart

The current task-specific `quickbooks/hx09-settlement-splits.patch` extends native `Transactions.jsx`, retaining its store and native persistence endpoint. Expanded bank rows expose Split. This opens a real modal titled Split Transaction, with the downloaded transaction description/date and amount, optional Payee selector, Category and Customer record selectors, Description, signed Amount (USD), row deletion, Add lines, Reset, Memo, Cancel and Save and add. Difference compares the signed line total to the downloaded deposit in exact cents. Incomplete/invalid accounts, invalid amounts, zero lines or a nonzero difference disable Save and add. Cancel and Reset do not post the transaction.

Categories resolve to existing account IDs and customers resolve to existing customer IDs. The saved rows retain both IDs and their display names, not arbitrary typed category/customer labels. The private verifier resolves them against trusted initial records. Save and add writes the split rows/memo through the original native updateTransaction/saveState path and moves the transaction from For Review to Categorized (stored status posted). Reopening the saved modal restores the selected records, descriptions, signed amounts and residual. This patch contains no expected settlement amounts, customer names, refund references or correct task selections.

## Shopify source and vendor counterpart

The standalone `shopify/hx09-refund-controls.patch` includes the already-existing variant stock layout correction plus the task-specific extension of native `OrderDetail.jsx`. The layout correction prevents Qty from sitting beneath Price at the task desktop width. Native StoreContext mutation and persistence remain in use.

Refund opens a full refund page with item quantity selection, default Restock items, optional shipping, internal Reason for refund, Summary, original-card/store-credit selector, editable bounded refund amount, default customer notification and Refund. Quantities cannot exceed the unrefunded line quantity; refund amount must be positive cents within the selected amount and remaining paid balance. Store credit, when selected, updates the actual customer credit balance. Original-method selection and successful status are persisted on the refund. Notification preference is retained in the mock refund and staff timeline; no live outgoing email service is claimed.

Restock items changes the actual matching tracked variant quantity through native product updates. Deselecting it leaves physical inventory for manual handling. Thus the approved saleable mug may be restocked during refund or manually; the damaged cup must explicitly remain excluded. The separately paid replacement order uses native fulfillment and the authorized manual inventory adjustment. No fabricated unified exchange dialog or second replacement payment is introduced.

Shopify's current primary procedure is https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/fulfillment/managing-orders/refunding-orders . It documents partial quantities, optional manual restocking, shipping, internal reason, refund method/amount and default notification. The new page implements the relevant documented choices.
