# ORCID desktop pilot

The initial record belongs to the fictional researcher Alex Morgan. Two works have fixed IDs. The journal article is self-reported and the dataset comes from an archive. Manually adding a work prepends it to `works` and records the researcher as its source. Cancel leaves the record unchanged. The visibility selector starts at the researcher's default.

The pilot covers the Works section and manual entry of title, type, year and visibility. The green Works header, selection controls and source rows follow the official desktop screenshots. Researchers may edit their own works and change visibility on individual or selected records. An organization-sourced title has no metadata edit action. Employment, peer review and identifiers remain outside this pilot. The authenticated page has been reconstructed from public documentation.
